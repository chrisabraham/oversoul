- **Status:** One of: client, former client, prospect, partner, vendor, plus a qualifier if needed (on hold, paused, cold). Date it: "Client (since Mon D, YYYY)"
- **Contact OK (YYYY-MM-DD):** ask owner
  <!-- Leave this as "ask owner" until the owner confirms. Then change it to yes or no and update the date. If no, add the reason on the same line (unsubscribed, deceased, dead address). -->
- **Who:** One or two lines. What the company does, the person's role, how they found you
- **Contact:** Email, phone, website, channel (email, Slack, Upwork room, Teams). Other people at the company and their roles
- **Deal:** Rate or price, structure (hourly, fixed, retainer, milestones), cap, platform and contract ID if any, start date
- **Scope:** What you're doing for them, in a line or two
- **Projects:** Links to this client's digests in projects/, if any. "None" if they have no PM project
- **Working rules:** How this person needs to be worked with, if anything specific
- **History:**
  - Date: What happened
- **Last contact:** Date and channel
- **Open threads:** Who owes what, with dates
- **Next step:** One line. What happens next, and who does it
